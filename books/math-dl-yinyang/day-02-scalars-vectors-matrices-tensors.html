4. Key Insight & Common Axis Traps

Trap 1: Channel vs. Batch Mix-up

In B × H × W × C, axis 0 selects an example, while axis 3 selects a measurement at the same pixel. Feeding B × C × H × W without permuting causes kernels to slide over channels instead of height & width.

Trap 2: Missing Batch Axis

Passing one unbatched 28 × 28 image into a layer expecting B × 28 makes the model treat the 28 rows of one digit as 28 separate training examples! Always add a leading batch axis (1 × 28 × 28 × 1).

Trap 3: Example vs. Feature Index

In xi,2, subscript i names the example and 2 names the second feature. Swapping rows and columns in a 10 × 4 data table turns features into fake examples.

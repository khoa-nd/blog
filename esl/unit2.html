    Grammar

    Order words

    What does it mean?
    Order words tell us the order things happen: what happens first, and what happens last.
    The Order
    First + Next + Then + After that + Finally
    The Pattern
    First + , + sentence
    Examples: A Wedding Day
    👰First, the bride got ready.
    🚗Next, the guests arrived.
    💍Then, the bride and groom got married.
    📷After that, the photographer took photos.
    🍽️Finally, everyone ate at the banquet.
    Examples: Make a Pop-up Card
    📄First, fold the paper in half.
    ✂️Next, cut two short lines.
    👉Then, push the middle part in.
    🎂Finally, draw a cake and write “Happy Birthday!”
    Remember!
    Put a comma , after the order word: First, …   Next, …   Finally, …
    Grammar

    before and after

    What does it mean?
    before = earlier, first.   after = later. They tell us when something happens.
    Look
    🧼 wash my hands
    before lunch
    ➜
    🍚 eat lunch
    middle
    ➜
    ⚽ play
    after lunch
    The Pattern
    sentence + before / after + time or event
    Examples
    🧼I wash my hands before lunch.
    ⚽I play after lunch.
    🪥I brush my teeth before bed.
    🎂Before the party, we made a cake.
    📷We took photos after the wedding.
    📚After school, I do my homework.
    Remember!
    At the start of a sentence, add a comma , → After school, I play.
    Grammar

    Describing people

    What does it mean?
    We can say how people feel, what they look like and what they are wearing.
    The Patterns (Click any row to listen)
    PatternExample
    He / She is + describing wordShe is happy. He is tall.
    He / She has + hair / eyesShe has long black hair.
    He / She is wearing + clothesHe is wearing a black suit.
    Examples
    👰The bride is wearing a white dress. She is happy.
    🤵The groom is wearing a black suit. He is proud.
    👵My grandmother has short grey hair. She is wearing a necklace.
    🧒My cousin is excited. He has big brown eyes.
    Feeling Words
    😊 happy 🔊 🤩 excited 🔊 🏅 proud 🔊 😌 peaceful = calm 🔊 ☺️ contented = happy and calm 🔊
    Remember!
    ✓ She is wearing a dress. She wearing a dress.

    Grammar

    Questions about the past

    What does it mean?
    To ask about the past, we use Did. After did, use the normal action word (go, eat, see).
    Yes / No Questions
    Did+ you+ go+ to the party?
    🎉Did you go to the wedding? — Yes, I did.
    🎂Did you eat cake? — No, I didn’t.
    👵Did your grandma come? — Yes, she did.
    Wh- Questions
    What / Where / Who / When+ did+ you+ do?
    🎁What did you do on your birthday? — I had a party.
    🏖️Where did you go? — I went to the beach.
    👴Who did you see? — I saw my grandfather.
    🪙What did the baby pick? — The baby picked a coin.
    📅When did your uncle get married? — Last year.
    Remember!
    ✓ Did you go? Did you went?   Answer with the past: I went …
    Grammar

    Why? to + action word

    What does it mean?
    We use to + action word to say why we do something.
    The Pattern
    I went to the shop+ to+ buy a present.
    Examples
    🛍️I went to the shop to buy a present.
    📷The photographer came to take photos.
    👵We went to Grandma’s house to say “Happy birthday!”
    ✂️I use scissors to cut paper.
    🧵We use string to make a necklace.
    💌I made a card to give to my mum.
    Ask “Why?”
    ❓Why did you go to the shop? — To buy a cake.
    ❓Why do you need a camera? — To take photos.
    Remember!
    After to, use the normal action word: ✓ to buy to bought to buying

    We use a title before a person’s name to be polite to adults.
    Which Title? (Click to listen)

    Msany woman (married or not married)Ms Park

    Mai   Tran
    Mai = my name (first name)    Tran = my surname (family name)
    Polite Greetings
    👋Good morning, Mr Lee.
    😊Hello, Mrs Smith. How are you?
    🤝Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.

    Remember!
    Titles, names and surnames start with a capital letter: Mrs Smith
    Grammar

    from, with, without, by

    from= the country or place it comes from
    🇹🇭I am from Thailand.
    👘Aiko is from Japan. Kimonos are from Japan.
    🌺A lei is from Hawaii.
    with= together, or using something
    👩I went to the wedding with my mum.
    ✂️I cut the paper with scissors.
    without= not with
    🍵I drink tea without sugar.
    🌧️He went out without his umbrella.
    by= who made it or did it
    💌This card was made by my sister.
    📷The photos were taken by the photographer.
    Remember!
    Ask Where are you from? → I am from Thailand. Countries start with a capital letter.

    Grammar

    Giving reasons: because

    What does it mean?
    We use because to give a reason. It answers the question Why?
    The Pattern
    sentence+ because+ reason
    Examples
    ☀️My favourite month is April because it is hot and sunny.
    🍃I like December because it is cool.
    🌧️I don’t like July because it is rainy.
    🎂I was excited because it was my birthday.
    🏅Grandma was proud because I tried hard.
    Asking and Answering
    ❓Why do you like May? — Because my birthday is in May.
    Weather Words
    🥶 cold 🔊 🍃 cool 🔊 🌤️ warm 🔊 🥵 hot 🔊 ☀️ sunny 🔊 💨 windy 🔊 🌧️ rainy 🔊
    Remember!
    Why? → because   Use because to join two ideas into one sentence.
    Grammar

    could and couldn’t

    What does it mean?
    could = I was able to do it in the past.   couldn’t (could not) = I was not able to do it.
    The Pattern
    I / He / She / We / They+ could / couldn’t+ action word
    Examples
    👶When I was a baby, I couldn’t walk.
    🏊When I was five, I could swim.
    👰At the wedding, I could see the bride.
    🧸I couldn’t find my teddy bear.
    🙈We played hide and seek. Nobody could find me!
    Asking and Answering
    ❓Could you read when you were four? — Yes, I could. / No, I couldn’t.
    Now and the Past
    NowIn the past
    I can ride a bike. I couldn’t ride a bike when I was three.
    Remember!
    After could, use the normal action word: ✓ could swim could swam
    Writing

    Joining sentences: and

    What does it mean?
    We use and to join two words or two short sentences. It makes our writing better.
    The Pattern
    sentence+ and+ sentence
    Two Sentences → One Sentence
    Two sentencesOne sentence with and
    I went to the beach. I played in the sand. I went to the beach and I played in the sand.
    Grandma made a cake. We ate it. Grandma made a cake and we ate it.
    The bride wore a dress. The groom wore a suit. The bride wore a dress and the groom wore a suit.
    Joining Words
    👩My aunt and uncle came to the party.
    😊I was happy and excited.
    🌸The bridesmaids threw petals and everyone smiled.
    Remember!
    No capital letter after and (only for names and I). One full stop at the very end.

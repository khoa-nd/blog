    Words

    Temperature

    What does it mean?
    Temperature tells us how hot or cold it is. We measure it in degrees Celsius (°C).
    The Pattern
    It is+ number+ degrees Celsius.
    How to Say It (Click any row to hear)
    We writeWe sayIt is …
    🥵45°Cforty-five degrees Celsiusvery hot
    🍃10°Cten degrees Celsiuscool
    🧊0°Czero degrees Celsiuscold – water turns to ice
    🥶-5°Cminus five degrees Celsiusvery cold
    Examples
    ☀️During the day, the desert is very hot. It is 45 degrees Celsius.
    🌙At night, the desert is cold. It can be minus 5 degrees Celsius.
    🌍The Sahara Desert is in Africa.
    🧊Antarctica is a cold desert. It is the coldest place in the world!
    Remember!
    ° = degrees    C = Celsius    minus – = colder than zero
    Grammar

    Describing words and opposites

    What does it mean?
    Describing words tell us more about a person, place or thing. They tell us what it is like.
    Two Patterns
    The desert+is+dry.
    It is+a+dry+desert.
    Examples
    🏜️The desert is hot and dry.
    🌵A cactus has sharp spines.
    🕯️A cactus has thick, waxy skin.
    🏞️The canyon is rocky.
    ⛰️The cliff is very steep.
    🦉The old man was wise and kind.
    Opposites (Tap to hear!)
    Remember!
    The describing word goes before the thing: ✓ a dry desert a desert dry
    Grammar

    Comparing two: bigger than

    What does it mean?
    We use -er + than to compare two things.
    The Pattern
    A camel+is+bigger than+a lizard.
    Spelling Rules (Click to hear)
    Most words: add -erfast → faster, small → smaller🔊
    Ends in e: add -rwide → wider, wise → wiser🔊
    Short word: double the last letterhot → hotter, big → bigger, thin → thinner🔊
    Ends in y: y → ierdry → drier, rocky → rockier🔊
    Special wordsgood → better, bad → worse🔊
    Examples
    🐪A camel is bigger than a lizard.
    🦅A hawk is faster than a mouse.
    ☀️The day is hotter than the night.
    🏜️The desert is drier than the forest.
    🐺Coyote is cleverer than Rattlesnake.
    Remember!
    Don’t forget than: ✓ bigger than a lizard bigger a lizard

    We use the + -est to compare three or more things. It means number 1, the most.

    🐭The mouse is the smallest.

    What does it mean?
    We use the past to tell a story about things that happened before.
    Regular Words: Add -ed (Tap to hear!)
    Special Words – Learn Them! (Tap to hear!)

    Grammar

    Questions about a story

    What does it mean?
    To ask about a story, use a question word + did. After did, use the normal action word.
    The Pattern
    Where+did+the friends+go?
    Examples
    🌴Where did the friends go? — They went to the oasis.
    ✍️What did he write in the sand? — He wrote sad words.
    🗣️Why did they argue? — Because they were hot and tired.
    😢How did he feel? — He felt hurt.
    🌙When did Coyote come? — He came in the evening.
    🐭Who did Coyote help? — He helped Mouse.
    Question Words
    Who? = person🔊
    What? = thing🔊
    Where? = place🔊
    When? = time🔊
    Why? = reason🔊
    How? = way / feeling🔊
    Remember!
    ✓ What did he write? What did he wrote?

    Grammar

    Pronouns: he / him, they / them

    What does it mean?
    Pronouns are short words we use instead of a name, so we do not say the name again and again.
    Two Kinds
    Before the actionAfter the action
    Ime🔊
    youyou🔊
    hehim🔊
    sheher🔊
    itit🔊
    weus🔊
    theythem🔊
    The Pattern
    He+helps+her
    Coyote helps Mouse.
    → He helps her.
    No Pronoun

    Coyote is clever. Coyote helps Mouse. Mouse thanks Coyote.

    With Pronouns
    Coyote is clever. He helps Mouse. She thanks him.
    Examples
    🐪The camel has a hump. It stores fat in it.
    🗣️The friends were angry. They argued.
    🐍Rattlesnake was trapped. Mouse helped him.
    📖My sister and I like stories. Mum reads to us.
    Remember!
    I is always a capital letter. Before the action: I, he, she, we, they. After: me, him, her, us, them.

    Writing

    Speech marks

    What does it mean?
    Speech marks “ ” show the exact words a person or animal says.
    The Patterns
    “Help!”+said Mouse.
    Mouse said,+“Help me!”
    The Rules
    1Put the spoken words inside “ ”.
    2Put , ! or ? inside the speech marks.
    3Start the spoken words with a capital letter.
    4New speaker? Start a new line.
    Examples: Rattlesnake, Mouse and Clever Coyote
    🐍“Please help me,” said Rattlesnake.
    🐭“You will eat me!” said Mouse.
    🐺“What happened?” asked Coyote.
    🐍“That is not fair!” yelled Rattlesnake.
    Saying Words
    said 🔊 asked 🔊 yelled 🔊 explained 🔊 answered 🔊
    Remember!
    Only the words that are spoken go inside the speech marks.

    Notes are short words to help us remember. In a report we write full sentences.

    Fun fact! It can drink a lot of water at one time.

    Remember!

    Writing

    Joining words: and, but, because

    and= adds more information
    🐪A camel has a hump and long eyelashes.
    🌵The cactus is green and it has sharp spines.
    but= a different or opposite idea
    ☀️The desert is hot during the day, but it is cold at night.
    🐍Snakes can’t walk, but they can slither.
    because= gives a reason (answers Why?)
    🦎Lizards lie in the sun because they need to get warm.
    🐺I like Coyote because he is clever.
    The Pattern
    sentence+and / but / because+sentence
    Remember!
    Use and to add. Use but for a different idea. Use because to say why.

    Words

    Shapes: sides and corners

    What does it mean?
    Shapes have sides (straight lines) and corners (where two sides meet).
    ShapeSidesCornersRemember
    circle00A circle is round.
    square44All 4 sides are the same.
    rectangle442 long sides and 2 short sides.
    triangle33It has 3 straight sides.
    cube6 square sides8A cube is like a box.
    The Pattern
    A triangle+has+three sides.
    Examples
    📐A square has four sides and four corners.
    ⚫A circle has no corners.
    📏Draw a straight line from the top to the bottom.
    Remember!
    Count the sides and corners to name a shape.

    Grammar

    I can see him, her, it, them

    What does it mean?
    After an action word like see, we use me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
    The Pattern
    I can see+him / her / it / them
    Before the actionAfter the actionExample
    hehimWhere is Tom? I can see him.
    sheherWhere is Mia? I can see her.
    ititCan you see the rabbit? Yes, I can see it.
    theythemCan you see the two faces? Yes, I can see them.
    ImeLook at me! Can you see me?
    Examples: Look Again!
    🏺Look at the picture. I can see a vase.
    👥Look again! Now I can see two faces. I can see them back-to-back.
    🦆Is it a duck or a rabbit? I can see it! It’s a duck.
    Remember!
    ✓ I can see him. I can see he.
    Grammar

    Describing faces: has, wears

    What does it mean?
    We use has for parts of a face or body, and wears / is wearing for things like glasses.
    He / She+has+a beard.
    He / She+wears / is wearing+glasses.
    Examples
    🧔The man has a long beard.
    👴My grandpa has a grey moustache.
    👓My teacher wears glasses.
    👩Today she is wearing red glasses.
    😊Look! I can see a face. It has two eyes, a nose and a mouth.
    My Body
    ☝️I have ten fingers. The ends are my fingertips.
    🧠My brain helps me think. Optical illusions trick my brain!
    ✍️I write with my right hand. I am right-handed.
    ✋My sister writes with her left hand. She is left-handed.
    Remember!
    I / you / we / they → have    he / she / it → has
    Writing

    Commas in a list

    What does it mean?
    When we write a list of three or more things, we put a comma , between them. We use and before the last thing.
    thing+,+ thing+,+ thing+and+thing.
    🔷I can see a square, a circle, a triangle and a dot.
    🐸The frog is green, brown and yellow.
    ✂️I need paper, scissors, glue and a pencil.
    👀In the picture I can see a man, a dog and a tree.
    🦓Animals can have spots, stripes and blotches.
    Two Things? No Comma
    ⚫I can see a circle and a square.
    Remember!
    No comma before and. ✓ red, blue and green red, blue, and, green
    Grammar

    How often? always … never

    What does it mean?
    These words tell us how often we do something.
    From All the Time to Not at All (Tap to hear!)
    always
    100%🔊
    usually
    80%🔊
    often
    60%🔊
    sometimes
    40%🔊
    never
    0%🔊
    I+always+action word
    I+am / is / are+never+describing word
    Examples
    🪥I always brush my teeth.
    🚶I usually walk to school.
    📚She often reads books.
    🎮We sometimes play games.
    🙏He is never impolite.
    ❓How often do you play football? — I often play football.
    Remember!
    The how-often word goes before the action word, but after am / is / are.
    Words

    Opposites with un- and im-

    What does it mean?
    We can put un- or im- at the start of some words. It means not. It makes the opposite.
    un- / im-+word=the opposite
    un-
    kind→unkind🔊
    happy→unhappy🔊
    tidy→untidy🔊
    fair→unfair🔊
    im-
    possible→impossible🔊
    patient→impatient🔊
    polite→impolite🔊
    Examples
    ❌It is impossible to lick your elbow!
    🧦My room is untidy. I need to tidy it.
    🙏Don’t be impolite. Say please and thank you.
    ⏳I am impatient when I wait for the bus.
    😞Tom was unhappy because he lost his ball.
    Remember!
    Learn which one: unkind, unhappy, untidy, but impossible, impatient, impolite
    Grammar

    Instructions for an experiment

    What does it mean?
    Instructions tell us what to do. They start with an action word. We use order words to show the steps.
    First,+action word+the rest.
    Experiment: The Floating Finger
    1️⃣First, hold your two pointing fingers in front of your eyes.
    2️⃣Next, put your fingertips together.
    3️⃣Then, look past your fingers at the wall.
    4️⃣Finally, move your fingers apart a little.
    🤯What can you see? A small finger is floating in the air! Your brain is confused.
    More Action Words for Experiments
    Remember!
    Start with the action word. We do not say “you”: ✓ Look at the wall. You look at the wall.
    Writing

    End marks: . ? !

    What does it mean?
    Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with an end mark.
    .full stop
    for telling sentences and instructions    		I can see a vase.
    Close one eye.
    ?question mark
    for questions    		What can you see?
    Can you see the two faces?
    !exclamation mark
    for surprise, strong feelings or loud words    		Wow! That’s impossible!
    Look again!
    Examples
    🧪We did an experiment.
    ❓Is it a duck or a rabbit?
    🤩Wow! I can see it now!
    ☝️Put your fingertips together.
    Remember!
    Use only one exclamation mark: ✓ Wow! Wow!!!
    Grammar

    Why? to + action word

    What does it mean?
    We use to + action word to say why we do something.
    Close one eye+to+see the trick.
    Examples: Experiments
    👁️Close one eye to see the trick.
    🔄Turn the picture around to find the other face.
    🔍Look again to see the hidden picture.
    🪞We use a mirror to look at the picture.
    Examples: Camouflage
    🐸The frog is green to hide in the leaves.
    🦋Animals use camouflage to hide from danger.
    🐯The tiger creeps slowly to catch its meal.
    ❓Why is the moth brown? — To blend in with the bark.
    Remember!
    After to, use the normal action word: ✓ to see to saw to seeing
    Grammar

    How animals hide: slowly, quietly

    What does it mean?
    Camouflage is when an animal’s colours and patterns help it blend in and hide. How words (-ly) tell us how animals move and hide.
    The tiger+creeps+slowly.
    Most words: add -lyslow → slowly, quiet → quietly, careful → carefully, patient → patiently🔊
    Ends in y: y → ilyeasy → easily, happy → happily🔊
    Examples
    🐯The tiger creeps slowly through the grass.
    🦋The moth hides quietly on the bark.
    🦎The lizard blends in easily with the rocks.
    🐸The frog waits patiently for its meal.
    🦓Zebras have stripes. They hide easily in a group.
    Remember!
    A how word usually comes after the action word: creeps slowly, hides quietly
    Words

    British and American English

    What does it mean?
    People speak English in many countries. British English and American English sometimes use different spellings or different words. Both are correct!
    🇬🇧 British🇺🇸 American
    Spelling Patterns

    -our → -or: colour → color, favourite → favorite

    -re → -er: centre → center, metre → meter

    Examples
    🇬🇧British: My favourite colour is grey.
    🇺🇸American: My favorite color is gray.
    Remember!
    In our class we use British English: colour, favourite, grey.
